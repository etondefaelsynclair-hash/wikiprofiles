Biography

Elon Reeve Musk is an entrepreneur born in South Africa who became known internationally through businesses involved in electric vehicles, commercial spaceflight, artificial intelligence, and online communication.

Musk founded SpaceX in 2002 and became a major early investor in Tesla in 2004. He has led both companies through different stages of growth and technological development. His other ventures have included Neuralink, The Boring Company, and online payment businesses.

His work has influenced discussions about electric transportation, reusable rockets, satellite internet, artificial intelligence, and the future of human spaceflight. His businesses and public statements have also attracted debate about working conditions, regulation, content moderation, and corporate governance.

Early Life and Education

Musk was born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa. He developed an early interest in computers and programming and reportedly created a video game as a young teenager.

At age 17, he moved to Canada. He later attended Queen's University in Ontario before transferring to the University of Pennsylvania in the United States.

At the University of Pennsylvania, Musk studied physics and economics. He subsequently enrolled in a graduate program at Stanford University but left shortly after beginning it to pursue opportunities in internet technology.

Zip2 and PayPal

In the 1990s, Musk co-founded Zip2, a company that developed online city guides and business directories. Compaq acquired Zip2 in 1999.

He later helped establish X.com, an online financial services company. Following a merger with Confinity, the business became associated with PayPal, which was acquired by eBay in 2002.

These early ventures gave Musk experience in internet businesses and provided capital for later investments in aerospace and electric transportation.

SpaceX and Space Exploration

Musk founded Space Exploration Technologies Corporation, known as SpaceX, in 2002. The company's stated goal has included reducing the cost of space transportation and developing systems for missions beyond Earth.

SpaceX developed the Falcon launch vehicle family and Dragon spacecraft. The company became known for recovering and reusing rocket boosters, as well as providing launch and cargo transportation services.

Its Starship program is designed around a fully reusable launch and transportation system intended for future missions to the Moon, Mars, and other destinations.

Tesla and Electric Vehicles

Musk joined Tesla as an early investor in 2004 and became its chief executive officer in 2008. Tesla develops electric vehicles, battery storage products, charging infrastructure, and energy generation technologies.

Under Musk's leadership, Tesla expanded its vehicle lineup and manufacturing operations. Its products have included the Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, and Cybertruck.

Tesla has played a significant role in the global electric vehicle market. The company has also faced scrutiny over vehicle safety, autonomous-driving claims, workplace practices, and its leadership.

Artificial Intelligence and X

Musk founded xAI in 2023 to develop artificial intelligence systems. Its work has included the Grok family of AI models and computing infrastructure for AI development.

In 2022, Musk acquired Twitter, the social media platform, which was subsequently renamed X. The platform underwent major changes to its branding, verification systems, moderation policies, and business strategy.

In 2025, X and xAI were combined in a corporate transaction. SpaceX subsequently announced the acquisition of xAI in 2026. Corporate structures and leadership arrangements can change, so readers should consult the companies' official announcements for the latest details.

Other Companies and Projects

Neuralink

Neuralink works on brain-computer interface technology. Its research includes systems designed to allow neural signals to interact with digital devices.

The Boring Company

The Boring Company develops tunnelling technologies and underground transportation projects intended to address certain urban transport challenges.

Solar energy

Musk was associated with SolarCity, a solar energy company acquired by Tesla in 2016. Tesla subsequently incorporated solar products and stationary battery storage into its broader energy business.

Leadership and Public Influence

Musk's business approach has involved ambitious engineering targets, vertically integrated operations, rapid product development, and substantial investment in manufacturing and infrastructure.

His companies operate in industries with significant technical, financial, regulatory, and environmental challenges. Their products and decisions have influenced public discussion about transportation, space exploration, energy, AI, and the role of private companies in technology.
