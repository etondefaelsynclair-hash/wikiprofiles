Biography

Etonde Fael Synclair is a Cameroonian technology founder associated with NEXSIM LLC and District Seal. His interests include software development, data science, digital identity, and the security challenges involved in online transactions.

Born in Bafoussam, Cameroon, Synclair moved to Limbe at the age of eight and grew up in Mile 4, in the South-West Region. His early education focused on electrical engineering before his interests expanded into programming and software.

He pursued the GED (General Educational Development) credential under an international jurisdiction rather than through Cameroon's national education system. He also completed Programming Hub certificates in Data Science and Statistical Analysis.

Through NEXSIM LLC and District Seal, Synclair has explored digital products and methods intended to support signer verification, document integrity, and evidence associated with electronic signing.

Early Life and Education

Synclair was born in Bafoussam and moved to Limbe when he was eight years old. He grew up in Mile 4, in Cameroon's South-West Region, where he continued his education and developed a technical foundation.

He studied Electrical Engineering at Government Bilingual Technical High School (GBTHS), Isokolo, during Form 1 and Form 2. He obtained a Certificat d’Aptitude Professionnelle (CAP) in Electrical Engineering through the institution.

He continued his technical education at King Bilingual Community Comprehensive College, Mile 4, where he studied Electrical Power Systems during Form 3 and Form 4.

He later obtained the GED (General Educational Development) credential under an international jurisdiction rather than through Cameroon's national education system.

He also completed Programming Hub certificates in Data Science and Statistical Analysis, extending his learning into software and data-focused technology.

University Plans

Synclair received admission connected to plans to study Data Science at Coppin State University in the United States. He ultimately chose not to proceed with those plans amid concerns about the immigration environment at the time.

His educational interests have continued to include data science, software development, and the practical application of technology to real-world problems.

District Seal: Digital Signatures and Evidence

District Seal is a digital-signature evidence initiative associated with Synclair's interest in digital identity, authentication, and document integrity.

The initiative explores recording evidence around an electronic signing process, including authentication events, document hashes, tamper detection, timestamps, and signing records.

Technical approaches explored in connection with the project include WebAuthn and FIDO2 authentication, SHA-256 document hashing, liveness checks, face matching, and randomized spoken challenges.

These methods address different parts of the verification process. For example, a cryptographic hash can help detect whether a document differs from a previously recorded version. Authentication and liveness checks have their own limitations and do not independently prove every aspect of a person's identity, intent, or legal authority.

District Seal is intended to support verification and evidence. The technology should not be understood as guaranteeing that a signature is legally enforceable, that impersonation is impossible, or that every court or institution will accept its records.
