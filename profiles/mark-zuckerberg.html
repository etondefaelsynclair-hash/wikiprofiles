Biography

Mark Elliot Zuckerberg is an American technology entrepreneur best known for co-founding Facebook, the social networking platform that grew into one of the world's major digital services. He is the co-founder, chairman, and chief executive officer of Meta Platforms.

Zuckerberg began developing social software while he was a student at Harvard University. Facebook launched in 2004 and expanded from a university-focused network into a worldwide platform. The business later expanded through acquisitions and the development of additional products.

In 2021, Facebook's parent company changed its name to Meta Platforms, reflecting a broader corporate focus on social technology, virtual and augmented reality, and other computing platforms.

Early Life and Education

Mark Zuckerberg was born on May 14, 1984, in White Plains, New York. He grew up in Dobbs Ferry, New York, in a family that encouraged education and intellectual curiosity.

As a teenager, Zuckerberg developed an interest in programming and created computer programs for communication and entertainment. One of his early projects was a music recommendation program called Synapse.

He attended Phillips Exeter Academy before enrolling at Harvard University in 2002. At Harvard, he studied psychology and computer science. He left the university before graduating to focus on building Facebook.

Founding Facebook

Facebook launched on February 4, 2004, initially serving students at Harvard. The service expanded to other colleges and eventually opened to a much wider public audience.

The platform enabled people to create profiles, connect with friends, share updates, and interact with communities. Its growth helped transform online social networking and digital advertising.

Facebook became a publicly traded company in 2012. Over time, it developed into a large technology business with international operations and a broad advertising platform.

Meta Platforms

In October 2021, Facebook announced that its parent company would be renamed Meta Platforms. Facebook remained the name of the social networking service, while Meta became the corporate identity.

Meta's portfolio includes Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and technologies related to virtual and augmented reality. The company has also invested in artificial intelligence and computing infrastructure.

Zuckerberg continues to lead Meta as chairman and CEO, overseeing its corporate strategy, products, and long-term technology investments.

Leadership and Business Strategy

Zuckerberg's leadership has been closely associated with rapid product development, global user growth, and the integration of social platforms into a large advertising business.

Meta's strategy has also involved substantial investment in artificial intelligence, virtual reality, and augmented reality. These investments represent attempts to develop new products and computing experiences beyond traditional social networking.

Zuckerberg's career has also drawn public scrutiny over privacy, content moderation, competition, and the societal effects of social media. These issues remain important parts of public discussion about Meta and its platforms.

Philanthropy and Public Initiatives

Zuckerberg and his wife, Priscilla Chan, established the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative in 2015. The organization supports work involving science, education, and other social challenges.

The initiative uses a range of philanthropic and investment approaches to support research and projects intended to address long-term societal issues.
